Noble Narwhal and Nettle

Noble Narwhal is a powerful ocean superhero who protects people from dangerous villains. Nettle is the loyal sidekick who uses plant powers to help Noble Narwhal defeat enemies.

Their Foes

  • Doctor Darkness
    • Major Power: Can stop time for two minutes.
    • Minor Power: Can make lights turn off.
  • Captain Chaos
    • Major Power: Can control powerful storms.
    • Minor Power: Can make butterflies appear.
